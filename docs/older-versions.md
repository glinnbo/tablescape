# Tablescape: older versions

What was new in the versions before the prototypes (moved out of the app's What's new list in Prototype 24).

## Beta 4.11.7

- The Spotify slider on the deck sets Tablescape's Spotify volume: a mood no longer jumps back to the old volume, and sliding no longer floods Spotify (moods then didn't start)
- A mood always starts from the beginning of a song (not where Spotify left that playlist or album)

## Beta 4.11.6

- Arrange the deck: remove one button with the × on it or the Delete key; the right-click menu shows again (it opened behind the window)

## Beta 4.11.5

- The volume keys look like sliders in Arrange the deck; a phone or tablet page reloads by itself after Tablescape is updated

## Beta 4.11.4

- Volume sliders for the phone and tablet deck: SFX (the soundboard) and Spotify (Arrange the deck → Volume sliders)

## Beta 4.11.3

- No more black bars flickering over animated maps: videos are drawn with the picture (Options → TV → Video on the TV)

## Beta 4.11.2

- Button deck: sounds from the library (the soundboard plays them once; ambience and music start or stop on the map)

## Beta 4.11.1

- Talk no longer makes the TV flicker: the room lights take their colours without capturing the TV window

## Beta 4.11

- Library: your folders. A tree under each category, a path to go back, subfolders as cards; inside a folder, names without the folder's name
- Search finds folder names too (a heading takes you there); the word tags are gone; the Tokens and Talk lists browse folders as well

## Beta 4.10.1

- Missing files: tokens deleted from the library that were only on a Ready list no longer raise the alarm; one click takes them off

## Beta 4.10

- Tokens: their pictures are drawn bigger than their squares (Medium 130 %), a size per 5e size in Options → Tokens
- A selected token: a thin ring round the creature at any zoom, a compact see-through toolbar (or docked); new Options → Tokens

## Beta 4.9

- New effect: Window light. Sunlight through a window on the floor, the shadows of its bars, dust in the beam; six windows, six kinds of bars
- The light shaft has soft edges on a dark map; the TV preview follows what changes on the TV instead of a clock

## Beta 4.8

- Dragging a map is lighter: your screen holds still meanwhile, nothing new loads, the TV preview waits
- Big animated assets get sharper frames (up to 1024 pixels); Sharp video plays one as the video itself

## Beta 4.7

- Talk and Tokens: the party and the Ready list; List … opens every portrait or token (click to use, tick to get it ready)
- Talk: Stand at is a slider; a new Party folder for the players' pictures, with a button to move them there

## Beta 4.6.3

- Options → Looks → Fights on the TV: turn order and round each on or off, how many, enemies as "Enemy"

## Beta 4.6.2

- A file renamed or moved while the campaign is open is pointed out when you come back to Tablescape, by name

## Beta 4.6.1

- The parchment of a new map no longer shows the map's name

## Beta 4.6

- Export a campaign or one chapter as one file: share it as an adventure, or a full copy for another PC
- Import a campaign on the start screen, or double-click the file; the little maid runs in a ring meanwhile

## Beta 4.5.1

- The Toolbox on its own (no heading); Grid → Tilt has an arrow back to 0°
- A held map moves on smoothly after a rest; the parchment Talk banner shows no name

## Beta 4.5

- Build → Map (was Move map) has Colours: presets, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth; hold to see the original; snapshots keep them
- Build in a new order; the Toolbox stands alone between Build and Play, with Snapshots in it
- Options → Looks: how a new map arrives and the look of Talk, each with Try on the TV
- New ink with splashes, droplets and drips; the parchment banner rolls away; painted effects follow the grid
- A held drag keeps the map until you let go; Room lights −20 % / +20 % (also hotbar and phone)

## Beta 4.4.1

- Light shaft: the dust can be much smaller now (Dust size from 0.1×)

## Beta 4.4

- Moving a map is smooth: it slides as one picture while you drag it, then it is drawn once
- A new map arrives under smoke, on parchment or through embers; Talk has the same looks (both now in Options → Looks)
- Room lights: Off switches the lamps off; Let go puts them back as they were
- Effects are sized by the grid square; the light shaft's dust size; Open Spotify next to the player

## Beta 4.3.1

- Every panel has a "?" in its title bar with its tips; the long explanations are gone from the panels
- Options: the reference picture only, the size of this window as a slider
- Initiative stays in the Toolbox; Map notes (Build) and the Session log (Toolbox) instead of two Notes
- Talk: portraits from 10 to 70 %, a taller list, + puts a portrait on the Ready list
- Removing a player asks first; messages stay inside the window beside a moved hotbar

## Beta 4.3

- Options (Ctrl ,): the TV, its picture, players, sound, performance, this screen and the library in one window; the Sound tool keeps Chapter, Map and Combat
- The hotbar in four shapes: 10 × 1, 5 × 2, 2 × 5 or 1 × 10 (right-click the hotbar)
- Toolbox in groups, Dice with the map tools; right-click a panel to pin it to the rail
- Fog: its handy tips behind the "?" in the title bar
- Phone and tablet: a clearer turn order with a bigger round; the button catalog stays open while you add
- A snapshot whose changes you saved no longer asks again
- New names: Button deck, Map light, Lamp scene

## Beta 4.2.3

- Stream Deck: give a button a colour of its own (Arrange the deck: select it, pick a colour); a light offers the colour it shows

## Beta 4.2.2

- Room lights: Off and quitting really put the lamps back (also after a test flash or a lamp set by hand), fades no longer dip, the Govee cloud is spared
- Player screen: unshown enemies stay "??" in a shared turn, and it hides the turn order when the TV does
- Character cards: a new AC column keeps the players' AC, renaming in the turn order renames the player, a removed portrait stays removed

## Beta 4.2.1

- Stream Deck: simple presets (only lines of buttons to add or take away) and one Custom for anything
- Character cards: pictures from anywhere on this PC (From this PC …), the picker fits its window

## Beta 4.2

- Character cards (campaign page → Players): portrait, Talk sprite, map token, colour, AC, HP, passives, senses, languages, notes, only for you
- Toolbox → Characters (Shift + I): the party at a glance, a passive check (who notices it), inspiration, exhaustion, death saves
- A tablet gets a deck of its own (Phone & tablet → Arrange the deck): the players with their cards, and your hotbar or your buttons

## Beta 4.1

- The Stream Deck on your phone is arranged at the PC now (Phone & tablet → Arrange the deck): presets, your own buttons or the hotbar, the turn order on top (tap it: next turn)
- Player screen: a tablet or another screen of this PC shows the party, and in a fight the turn order with pictures; enemies are "??" until you show them (right-click in the turn order)

## Beta 4.0.1

- Your screen: size of everything (Options → This screen, or Ctrl + / Ctrl − / Ctrl 0); the TV stays as it is
- The map bar stays where it was scrolled to when you open a map
- Typing no longer triggers shortcuts: "?" in the problem note, keys behind the open Guide, the moment after Enter in a field; Enter in a turn-order column goes one row down

## Beta 4.0

- Phone & tablet (Toolbox): your phone or tablet as a remote on the same Wi-Fi, connected with a QR code
- Pages of tiles you arrange on the device: buttons for anything on the hotbar, the initiative with next turn and HP, room lights, sound and Spotify, and your hotbar itself

## Beta 3.4

- Room lights on the hotbar work now: Table, Room light, Off and your light scenes
- Lamps beside the table: their side of the TV (like Ambilight) or the whole TV

## Beta 3.3

- Room lights setup in five steps: find, connect, a plan of your room (table, TV, your seat; drag the lamps to where they stand), groups, try it
- Room lights panel: Table, Scene, Room light, Off; light scenes made on the plan, on the hotbar too
- No flashing (soft colour swells instead), and the lamps go back to how they were on Off and when Tablescape closes

## Beta 3.2

- Room lights in two groups: drag lamps into Ambience (the colours on the TV, or the mood colours) and Effects (the flashes); a lamp can be in both

## Beta 3.1

- Room lights → Test and tune: each lamp by hand with its own limits, your own colour for every mood (Day to Cave, Fight, Break, Lights out), every effect to try or switch off

## Beta 3

- Room lights (prototype), in the Toolbox: Govee lamps (over your network or the Govee cloud) and IKEA bulbs (TRÅDFRI gateway) follow the light of the map, fights, breaks and moments, and flash for lightning, explosions, spells and natural 20s

## Beta 2.4

- Painted blood is uneven now: thick and thin, lumps and runs at the edges, drops of every size, trails of drops and wider spatter

## Beta 2.3

- The "Preparing animations" pill sits below the bar at the top (Send to table, aiming) instead of covering it

## Beta 2.2

- Gusts roll across the table: every few seconds a gust front passes the way the wind blows, and rain, snow, sand, leaves and wind rush ahead where it is
- Weather speed goes up to 10× (1× sits in the middle of the slider)
- Changing a layer's speed or direction no longer makes it jump; the sandstorm no longer flickers

## Beta 2

- New painted effects: ice, burning ground, webs, glowing ground (any colour), grass in the wind, oil. An oil slick's card can set it alight
- New placed effects: rune circle, portal, light shaft, crackling arcs, vent (steam, gas or fire bursts), falling water. Light shaft and arcs turn with Facing or Ctrl + wheel
- The bullseye lantern: a cone of light that faces the way you turn it (carried: the way the token faces). Every light can pulse or sputter
- Weather: underwater light, bubbles, wind, heat shimmer, and the mixes Underwater, Desert heat and Gale
- Moments: teleport, smoke puff, lights out (every light dies one by one), rockfall, flood
- Spell looks water, earth and swarm, with Watery Sphere, Tidal Wave, Maelstrom, Erupting Earth, Earthquake, Wall of Ice and Wall of Force
- Lava, burning ground, glowing ground and placed magic light up a dark map

## Beta 1

- A half see-through preview while placing assets: where it lands and how big, also for animated ones and when dragging from the library or the hotbar
- The processor draws the animations now (the graphics card stuttered with many of them); the benchmark "Graphics card vs processor" measures both on your PC and keeps the better one
- A ceiling for the memory animations may take, from the computer's memory, so a big map never ends in a blank window
- A window that stops is loaded again by itself, with a note; the table opens on the TV again when the TV comes back
- A damaged campaign or map file is shown as such, never written over, with Earlier versions one click away; a failed save says so
- Where the library lives is asked at the first start, shown under Options → Library, and can be moved
- This guide, with ? on every screen, and Report a problem
- Performance settings say what they do; "baked" is "prepared" now

## Alpha 2.5

- Animations prepared at their full speed, in pieces, so long ones take little memory
- The benchmark measures the animations themselves
- The save question for snapshots waits for an answer
- Deleting a campaign asks first; Recently deleted brings it back

## Alpha 2.3 – 2.4

- Animated assets prepared once and drawn from frames on both screens (the little maid shows it)
- The benchmark
- Long names wrap; the Layers panel fixed
